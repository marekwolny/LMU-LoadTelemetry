Braking zones — deltas to the reference

Zones less than 60 m apart are merged (two-stage braking). Δ braking: negative = brakes earlier (shorter distance), positive = later. Δ throttle: negative = gets on the throttle earlier. Rival data refreshes at ~5 Hz (0.2 s), so treat deltas < 10–15 m as approximate.
